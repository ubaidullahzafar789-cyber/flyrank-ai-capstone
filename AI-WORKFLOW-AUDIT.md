# AI Workflow Audit and Tool Setup

## Purpose

This audit maps recurring activities in my work as a Software Engineering student and AI-assisted developer. It distinguishes work that must remain mine from work where AI can assist, after review, or support a repeatable workflow. The classifications describe appropriate responsibility boundaries; they do not claim that every workflow has already been implemented.

## Three FL-02 to FL-04 Target Tasks

These are specific recurring tasks selected from the audit because they have repeatable inputs, review points, and measurable outputs.

### 1. Debugging Programming Errors

- **Task:** Debugging programming errors.
- **Current workflow:** Reproduce the error, inspect the relevant code and message, form a hypothesis, test a change, and verify the result.
- **AI role:** Help interpret error messages, suggest a small set of hypotheses, identify relevant code to inspect, and propose focused diagnostic steps. AI must not be treated as proof that a fix works.
- **What "done well" means:** The cause is explained in plain language, the smallest reasonable fix is identified, the fix is tested, and no unrelated code is changed.
- **Measurable success criteria:** For each of the first five uses, produce a written cause-and-fix summary; verify the proposed fix with a reproducible test or run; and require no more than one revision of the AI suggestion before reaching a verified result.

### 2. Creating Project Documentation

- **Task:** Creating project documentation.
- **Current workflow:** Gather the project purpose, current status, known setup steps, and limitations, then organize them into a concise Markdown document.
- **AI role:** Draft an outline, identify missing sections, improve clarity, and check for unsupported claims. I provide the facts and approve the final wording.
- **What "done well" means:** A reader can understand the project status and next step without being led to believe that unimplemented work is complete.
- **Measurable success criteria:** For the next three documentation updates, complete a first draft within 30 minutes, include zero unsupported claims after review, and require no more than one revision cycle before publishing.

### 3. LinkedIn and Resume Improvement

- **Task:** LinkedIn and resume improvement.
- **Current workflow:** Select an accurate experience or project detail, review its wording, and revise the presentation for clarity and relevance to an opportunity.
- **AI role:** Suggest concise wording, flag vague statements, compare a draft with a provided role description, and identify claims that need evidence. AI must not add achievements, tools, or experience that I did not provide.
- **What "done well" means:** The final version is accurate, specific, readable, and tailored without overstating experience.
- **Measurable success criteria:** For each of the next three targeted revisions, produce one reviewed draft within 30 minutes, retain zero unsupported claims, and record at least three concrete wording improvements or remove the draft if no evidence-based improvement is found.
